# EDA report

In this report, we identify different patterns observed in the data used.

## ICP dataset

ICP dataset can be analyzed in two subsections:
    
    * Tree level (species, defoliation, growth, diameter, observation period)
    * Plot level (locations, country, soil solutions, depositions, social class, defoliation)

- Mainly four species in the dataset (Spruce, Pine, Beech and Oak).
- The species are distributed in 342 plots.
- Among these plots, there are 71 (21%) plots with multiple species, i.e. there is a mix of species in these plots.
